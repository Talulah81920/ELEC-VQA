\section*{Data Availability}
The ELEC-VQA dataset and code are openly available at \url{https://github.com/Talulah81920/ELEC-VQA}. The accompanying materials comprise schematic images, source-grouped annotations, aligned supervision files, controlled-change pairs, and evaluation code. The dataset is available under CC BY 4.0 and the original project code under the MIT License; third-party materials retain their respective terms.
